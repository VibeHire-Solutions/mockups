Formerly Saw Endodontic Clinic, still specialist-led.

Affinity Dental Clinic has been treating patients from Damansara City Mall since 1993, when it operated as Saw Endodontic Clinic. That root canal expertise is still the core of the practice — now alongside a full specialist team covering implants, gum treatment, prosthodontics and oral surgery.

Every case is approached from a multi-disciplinary view, so treatment planning draws on more than one specialist's judgment before it reaches the chair.
